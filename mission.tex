\begin{rubric}{MISSION ALIGNMENT \& VISION}
\entry*[]
My ultimate professional commitment is to the structural, human, and equitable realization of a Viksit Bharat. I believe that sustainable national transformation cannot be achieved solely through top-down macroeconomic indexing; it requires a compassionate, inclusive narrative that translates policy blueprints into resilient, people-centric public infrastructure. Driven by an enduring interest in literature and narrative documentation, my goal is to understand the citizens behind the statistics, honor their lived experiences, and bring an empathetic, ground-level lens to administrative frameworks. I intend to dedicate my clinical expertise, literary voice, and grassroots perspective to platforms like NITI Aayog to help design public policy that ensures true human flourishing from the ground up.
\end{rubric}
